Shift Coordinator, Northgate Logistics (fictional)
Location: Memphis, TN (onsite)

About the role: Coordinate outbound shifts at our 300,000 square foot distribution center. You will supervise a team of 25 associates.

Schedule: Shifts are posted three days in advance. Rotating shifts include nights and weekends. Overtime is mandatory during peak season and may be assigned with less than 24 hours notice.

Phone and communication: Most of your day is spent on radio and phone calls with carriers, vendors, and the floor. A headset is required.

Responsibilities: Assign tasks, track productivity against hourly targets, and resolve issues on the floor. Other duties may be assigned by management.

Leave: Employees accrue 5 days of PTO in their first year. Requests for time off should be submitted at least 30 days in advance. Requests during peak season may be denied.

Work environment: The floor is loud and fast. Dress code requires steel-toe boots and a high-visibility vest at all times.

Interview process: On-site interview with the operations director, followed by a practical walk-through of the floor.
