Data Analyst (Remote), Tidewater Insurance Group (fictional)

We are a fully remote, async-first team across four time zones. Most decisions are documented in writing and shared in our project wiki before any meeting takes place.

What you will do: Build dashboards and analyses that support underwriting decisions. Own the monthly reporting cycle end to end.

How we communicate: We use Slack for day-to-day questions and a weekly written update in place of status meetings. Live calls are rare and always scheduled in advance.

Expectations: Each quarter, you will receive a written set of goals with clear success measures. Your manager reviews progress with you in a one-on-one every two weeks.

Working hours: You set your own hours within a shared overlap window of 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM Eastern. Flexible hours are the norm for this team.

Leave and benefits: Unlimited paid time off with a minimum of two weeks per year. Our written leave policy covers sick days, family leave, and caregiver leave, and it is available in our handbook.

Interview process: Recruiter screen, a take-home analysis of about three hours, and one structured interview with a panel that uses the same questions for every candidate.

Equal opportunity: we are an equal opportunity employer. Adjustments to the hiring process or the role are available on request; contact the recruiting team to ask.
