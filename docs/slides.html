Between the Lines

Know what a job asks of you before you apply.

The problem

Postings hide what decides fit

Hours, pace, sick days, a child at home: most postings leave them out, so people guess, or stay silent.

What is new

Every claim has a source

Quoted

Each claim is a quote from the posting. Invented quotes are rejected.

Repeatable

Scores come from fixed code. Repeat checks matched exactly.

Private

Questions are checked against your notes. Every change is logged.

How the agents work

Two language-model agents, three checks
